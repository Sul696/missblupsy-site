About Miss Blupsy

A small company making a body for AI on your desktop.

What we make

Miss Blupsy is a free desktop companion for Windows, macOS and Linux: an animated bubble character that gives your AI a voice and a body. You talk to her in Arabic, English and 12 more languages, and Claude (or her free AI brains) does the work on your computer through her tools, with clear permissions and a confirmation before anything risky. She shows each step live in her speech bubble.
