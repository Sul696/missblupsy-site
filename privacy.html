Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026. Applies to the Miss Blupsy app, its extension for AI apps (MCP), and this website.

In short

Miss Blupsy runs on your computer. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics: we do not collect, receive or store any of your data. What she sees, hears and does stays on your computer, except where you send it somewhere yourself, as described below.

Your voice

When you call her to listen, your voice is turned into text on your computer (with the open Whisper model). Your voice is never sent anywhere. The speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face the first time you use listening.

Optional online hearing (off by default, in the Hearing menu): if you turn it on, each recording is sent to Groq with your own Groq key to be turned into text, under Groq's privacy policy. If Groq cannot answer, she hears you on your computer as above. Each time Groq is asked is noted in the activity log, without the recording or the text.

The weather: when you ask her about the weather, only the name of the place (or, if you name none, the city of your computer's time zone) is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a free weather service with no account. Nothing else about you is sent.

Optional AI brains

If you add your own keys for her brains (Claude from Anthropic, Google Gemini, GitHub Models, Mistral, Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter or your Azure OpenAI), what you ask her, and the screenshots and file contents she reads to do it, are sent to those services to be answered, under their own privacy policies. Your keys are stored encrypted with your operating system's keychain.

Your screen

She takes a picture of your screen only when the AI app you use asks her to, and only with your permission (you choose "ask me", "allow" or "never"). She never captures windows of banking or password apps. The picture is handed to the AI app you are using (for example Claude), which handles it under that app's own privacy policy.

Files

She reads, edits or opens files only inside the folders you allow, and keeps a backup before any edit, on your computer. File contents she reads are handed to the AI app you are using, as above.

Your other computers

If you link your own computers ("My devices"), files and requests travel directly between them on your local network, end-to-end encrypted (X25519 + AES-256-GCM). Nothing goes through any server of ours.

Optional natural voice

By default she speaks with your computer's own voice. If you choose a natural voice and enter your own key from Google Gemini, Microsoft Azure, OpenAI or ElevenLabs, the text of what she says is sent to that provider to be spoken, under that provider's privacy policy. Your key is stored encrypted with your operating system's keychain.

What stays on your computer

  • Your settings, what she learned (in ~/.miss-blupsy/memory.json), and backups of edited files.
  • An activity log of the actions she took (~/.miss-blupsy/activity.log), without the text you typed.

You can delete any of these at any time. Uninstalling the app and deleting the ~/.miss-blupsy folder removes everything.

The AI app you connect

Miss Blupsy is a tool that AI apps use. What you say to the AI app, and what she hands to it, is handled by that app (for example Anthropic for Claude) under its own privacy policy, not by us.

This website

No cookies and no tracking. It remembers your chosen language in your own browser only. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and downloads come from GitHub, which see your IP address like any website you visit.

Children

Miss Blupsy is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue. If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with its date.

سياسة الخصوصية (بالعربي)

باختصار: Miss Blupsy تشتغل على جهازك. ما عندنا خوادم ولا حسابات ولا إحصائيات، وما نجمع أي بيانات منك.

  • صوتك: يتحوّل لنص على جهازك نفسه، وما يطلع منه. موديل السمع ينزل مرة وحدة من Hugging Face.
  • السمع عبر الإنترنت (اختياري، مقفل افتراضياً): لو شغّلته من قائمة السمع، كل تسجيل يروح لـ Groq بمفتاحك عشان يتحول لنص، حسب سياسة Groq. ولو ما ضبط، تسمعك على جهازك.
  • الطقس: لما تسألها عن الجو، يروح اسم المكان بس (أو مدينة المنطقة الزمنية لجهازك لو ما حددت مكان) لـ Open-Meteo، خدمة طقس مجانية بدون حساب. وما يروح أي شي ثاني عنك.
  • عقولها (اختياري): لو حطيت مفاتيحك (Claude من Anthropic وGemini وGitHub وMistral وGroq وغيرها)، طلبك وما تقراه عشان تسويه (صور الشاشة والملفات) يروح لهالخدمات عشان ترد، حسب سياساتها. والمفاتيح محفوظة مشفّرة في جهازك.
  • شاشتك: ما تصوّرها إلا لما يطلب تطبيق الذكاء وبإذنك، وما تصوّر نوافذ البنوك وكلمات السر أبداً. والصورة تروح لتطبيق الذكاء اللي تستخدمه (مثل Claude) حسب سياسته.
  • الملفات: داخل المجلدات اللي تسمح فيها بس، وتاخذ نسخة احتياطية على جهازك قبل أي تعديل.
  • أجهزتك: النقل بينها مباشر على شبكة بيتك ومشفّر، وما يمر على أي خادم.
  • الصوت الواقعي (اختياري): لو حطيت مفتاحك من Google أو Azure أو OpenAI أو ElevenLabs، نص كلامها يروح لهالشركة عشان تنطقه، والمفتاح محفوظ مشفّر في جهازك.
  • الموقع: بدون كوكيز وبدون تتبّع.
